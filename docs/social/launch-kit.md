## Day −7 · LinkedIn (page) · Sharp thinking · T1 card

**Hook:** I stopped asking AI for things. I started briefing it.

**Body:**
Most people type a vague sentence into a blank box and blame the AI for a vague answer. The problem was never the AI. It was the input.

Before any job, two questions: what do you need, and what will you decide with it? Twenty seconds. Clear in, clear out.

That habit is worth more than any new tool.

**CTA:** What's the last thing you asked AI for — and what did you actually decide with it?

_Alt hooks:_ "The blank box is the problem." · "Garbage in, garbage out is a user problem, not an AI problem."
