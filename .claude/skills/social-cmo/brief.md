# all41 social — account-prep brief

Status: **draft, awaiting founder answers**. Updated 2026-09-12.

## Decided (from the repo)

- Product: all41, `https://all41.app`. Live: chat + briefing, 3 apps, schedules, own files with sources, $2 free credit.
- Positioning: clarity before AI. "Tell us the job. We'll handle the AI." Anti-line: "Other AI tools let you be lazy. all41 makes you sharp."
- Audience: solo operators — founders, consultants, agency owners, freelancers. Not teams yet, not developers, not enterprise.
- Visual: dark, Space Grotesk / DM Sans / JetBrains Mono / Instrument Serif italic, traffic-light phases, one phase colour per artboard.
- Money: plain dollars, mono type, price before run, $0.05–$0.25 typical, $297/mo sprawl stack.
