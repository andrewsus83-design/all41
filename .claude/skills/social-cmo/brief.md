# all41 social — account-prep brief

Status: **decided**. Updated 2026-09-12.

## Founder decisions (2026-09-12)

- **Platforms:** all of them. LinkedIn + X are the core (buyers + build-in-public). Instagram for visual proof. TikTok + YouTube Shorts + Threads as reuse channels from the same footage/posts.
- **Language:** English first. Optional Bahasa variant of money-posts later if an ID audience shows up; not required now.
- **Accounts:** **Brand account only** — everything posts from the all41 handles. No personal founder profile in the mix, so we lose the personal-reach multiplier; compensate with stronger hooks, screen recordings, and consistency.
- **Camera:** not committed. Plan is no-face: text, cards, and screen recordings with voiceover/captions. TikTok/Reels/Shorts are all screen-recording based, not talking-head.
- **Market + budget:** US/EU English primary market, **small paid budget** — organic-first, with small boosts behind the best-performing sprawl-math and launch posts.

## Decided (from the repo)

- Product: all41, `https://all41.app`. Live: chat + briefing, 3 apps, schedules, own files with sources, $2 free credit.
- Positioning: clarity before AI. "Tell us the job. We'll handle the AI." Anti-line: "Other AI tools let you be lazy. all41 makes you sharp."
- Audience: solo operators — founders, consultants, agency owners, freelancers. Not teams yet, not developers, not enterprise.
- Visual: dark, Space Grotesk / DM Sans / JetBrains Mono / Instrument Serif italic, traffic-light phases, one phase colour per artboard.
- Money: plain dollars, mono type, price before run, $0.05–$0.25 typical, $297/mo sprawl stack.

## Paid plan (small budget)

Boost only after a post proves itself organically (top ~20% by saves+comments in 48 h). Priority to boost: The Bill (sprawl math), launch T3 carousel, 20-Seconds reel. Objective: profile visits / link clicks, not reach. Audience: solo founders / consultants / agency owners, US + EU, English. Cap per post small; kill under a click-through floor after 3 days.
