# all41 — brand reference for social

Everything here is lifted from the repo. If the repo changes, this file is wrong; re-read the sources listed in SKILL.md.

## One line

> You describe the job. all41 optimizes your context, picks the right AI, does the work, and tracks the cost. One chat. Done.

Public hero: **"Tell us the job. We'll handle the AI."**
Sub: *No prompts to learn. No tools to juggle. Describe the job in plain words; all41 picks the right AI, does the work, and shows the price before it runs. Start with $2 free.*

## Anti-positioning (the edge)

> Other AI tools let you be lazy. all41 makes you sharp.

all41 does not compete on which AI is smartest. It competes on **output quality**, which comes from input quality, which comes from forcing the user to think clearly before anything runs. That is the whole brand: **clarity before AI**.

## The cycle (every job, three lights)

| Light | Name | What happens | Colour |
|---|---|---|---|
| Red | Stop & Think | What do you need, and what will you decide with it | `#ff4d4f` |
| Amber | Prepare | How fresh, what tone, what shape; pulls in your own files | `#ffb020` |
| Green | Go & Track | See the price, run it, keep it; once, scheduled, or saved as an app | `#2fd27d` |

Use the three lights as the brand's visual and narrative spine. A carousel can literally be three slides: red, amber, green.

## Who we talk to

Primary: solo operators who already pay for several AI tools. Founders, consultants, agency owners, freelancers. They run 5–30 projects or clients and spend $200–500/mo on tools.
Secondary (later): small teams. Do not market to them yet.
Not: enterprise, developers.

Two framings the site uses, both valid on social:
- **New to AI?** — "You don't have to learn it."
- **Tired of juggling tools?** — "One place. One balance."

## What is true today (claimable)

- Chat + briefing flow (What → Goal → Condition → Execute → Track)
- 3 live apps: Morning Briefing (replaces Feedly / Mention), Competitor Crawler (replaces SEMrush / Ahrefs), Content Pipeline (replaces Jasper / Buffer)
- Any app runs once, daily, weekly or monthly; result to chat, email, or dashboard
- Own files and notes, linked; every answer shows its sources; second check on important jobs
- Price before every run; most tasks $0.05–$0.25; no subscription
- $2 free credit per verified email, no card
- Credits expire after 12 months; unused top-up refundable; failed runs not charged
- AIs tested daily per job type; the user never picks
- AI services used directly, no middleman (changelog 2026-09-12)
- Keys locked server-side; user never sees a key field

## Voice rules

**Do**
- Short declarative sentences. Contractions fine.
- Second person. "You" is the subject of most sentences.
- Name real tools and real dollar figures.
- Ask the reader a real question they can answer in a reply.
- Let dry humour in. No exclamation marks doing the work.
- Use the reflective serif line (italic) as a closing beat in visuals: *"Other AI tools let you be lazy. all41 makes you sharp."*

**Don't**
- Hype words: revolutionary, game-changing, supercharge, unleash, 10x, magic.
- Vendor words in public: model, LLM, API, token, prompt, RAG, embeddings, pipeline (the app name "Content Pipeline" is fine).
- Emoji strings, hashtags as sentences, "excited to announce".
- Capitalise all41. Never "All41" or "ALL41".
- Promise outcomes we do not control ("double your revenue").
- Compare with named competitors *as products*; compare with the *bill* instead.

**Words we use** — job, task, run, price, balance, plain words, sharp, sources, today's pick, one chat, apps, routine, schedule.
**Words we replace** — prompt → "describe the job"; model → "AI"; execute → "run"; credits → "balance" or "$"; workflow → "routine"; RAG → "your own files and notes".
